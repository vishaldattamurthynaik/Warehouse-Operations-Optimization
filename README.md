# Warehouse Operations Efficiency & Inventory Optimization

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes supply chain and warehouse data to identify potential inventory risks, evaluate supplier performance, and improve production planning using Python, data analysis, and Power BI.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze sales, production, revenue, and inventory levels.
* Identify products with potential inventory risks.
* Compare supplier manufacturing costs, lead times, and defect rates.
* Visualize key performance indicators using Power BI.
* Explore machine learning techniques for sales and defect-rate prediction.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **Python** – Data analysis and preprocessing
* **Pandas & NumPy** – Data manipulation
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Data visualization
* **Scikit-learn** – Machine learning experiments
* **Jupyter Notebook** – Analysis and experimentation
* **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development

## 📊 Key Findings

* Total revenue analyzed: approximately 577.60K.
* Total products sold: 46,099.
* Total production volume: 56,784.
* Average lead time: 17.08.
* Average defect rate: 2.28.
* 14 out of 100 SKUs met the project's rule-based potential inventory-risk criteria.

## 📈 Dashboard

The Power BI report includes:

* Executive KPI dashboard
* Revenue and product performance analysis
* Supplier quality and cost comparison
* Inventory and SKU risk analysis
* Business findings and recommendations

## 🤖 Machine Learning

Linear Regression and Random Forest models were explored for sales and defect-rate prediction. The initial results showed limited predictive performance, highlighting the need for more historical data and additional relevant features.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Review products with high production, low sales, and high stock.
* Compare supplier costs alongside lead times and quality.
* Monitor product-level inventory and sales performance.
* Improve production planning using demand and stock information.

## 📁 Project Structure

* `data/` – Original and processed datasets
* `notebooks/` – Jupyter Notebook analysis
* `dashboard/` – Power BI dashboard

## ⚠️ Limitations

The dataset contains 100 records and does not provide a time series. Therefore, inventory-risk flags are indicative, and the machine learning results should be treated as exploratory rather than reliable production forecasts.
